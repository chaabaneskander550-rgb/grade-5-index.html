✅ Affirmative
I / You / He / She / We / They + used to + verb
We used to play football on Thursday.
❌ Negative
subject + didn't use to + verb
They didn't use to have the internet.
⚠️ Careful!
After didn't we say use, not used.
✔ didn't use to   ✘ didn't used to
We use used to for past habits and situations that are not true now.
